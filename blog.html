Three local SEO checks for small businesses

Start with the information a customer needs when they find you.

  1. Check your opening hours. Compare your website with your Google Business Profile, especially around holidays.
  2. Make your service area clear. Say where you work and which services you offer. Use the place names your customers recognise.
  3. Try contacting yourself on mobile. Open your site on your phone and check whether your phone number, enquiry link or directions are easy to find and use.

The questions customers ask before they buy are a good place to start.

Pick one question you’ve answered more than once. Put a direct answer at the top, then explain what changes the answer and give a concrete example. End by linking to the relevant service so readers know what to do next.
